About this guide

An independent English guide to buying in Batumi, part of the Brik project.

Last updated: November 2026

What this is

Batumi/Real Estate is an English guide for people thinking of buying property in Batumi. It brings prices, areas, rules and taxes together in plain language.

The guide is part of Brik, a property platform for Georgia. Listing links and the request form lead to Brik's catalogue and agency.

How we work

  • Every figure names its source and period.
  • We do not mix price series from different analysts in one comparison.
  • Legal and tax pages are reviewed by Brik's partners in Georgia.
  • This is general information, not legal advice.
