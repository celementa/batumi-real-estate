Batumi real estate: what it costs and how foreigners buy

Foreigners can buy apartments in Georgia on the same terms as Georgian citizens, with no visa or residence needed. In Batumi, more than seven in ten buyers in surveyed new developments were foreign in the first eight months of 2026.

Who buys in Batumi

Among developers surveyed by Galt & Taggart for January–August 2026, Georgian citizens made 28% of purchases. The largest foreign groups were buyers from Ukraine, Russia and Belarus (19%), Europe (17%) and Israel (12%).

Foreign demand is why Batumi behaves differently from Tbilisi, where buyers are mostly local.

Six steps to buy

  1. Choose area and type

    Decide between resale and new build, and what the apartment is for. Areas

  2. Check the registry

    The Public Registry shows the owner and any mortgage or arrest on the property.

  3. Sign a preliminary agreement

    Price, timeline, deposit and when the deposit is refundable.

  4. Open a bank account

    Payments usually go through a Georgian bank, which may ask where the money comes from.

  5. Sign the sale contract

    At a notary or a Public Service Hall, in front of a registrar.

  6. Register ownership

    Ownership passes when the registry records it, usually within one to several working days. Full guide
