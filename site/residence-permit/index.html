Residence permit through property

Since 1 March 2026, owning property in Georgia worth at least $150,000 lets you apply for a one-year renewable residence permit. In Batumi that means roughly 47 to 87 m² at average new-build prices.

Last updated: November 2026

$150,000minimum market value of non-agricultural propertysince 1 March 2026; was $100,000
1 yearpermit term, renewable while you own the propertyEIG Law
up to 30 daysstandard processing; faster for a higher feeMadloba, October 2026

Conditions

  • You own non-agricultural property in Georgia registered in the Public Registry.
  • Its market value is at least $150,000, confirmed by a licensed valuer. The contract price is not what counts.
  • Several properties can be combined.
  • Your spouse and children can be included.

The permit is not automatic. The Public Service Development Agency decides, and the first application is usually made in person at a Public Service Hall.

Limits

  • This short-term permit does not lead to permanent residence.
  • Holders who qualified under the old $100,000 rule can keep renewing on the old terms while they keep that property.
  • A separate investment residence permit requires at least $300,000.
  • State fees depend on processing speed; see sda.gov.ge.
