Batumi areas: where to buy

Old Batumi

$3,224/m² average, turnkey primary market · 839 sales, Jan–Aug 2026

The historic centre around Europe Square and the old part of the seafront boulevard. Few new projects fit here, so prices are the highest in the city: some sold above $5,000 per m² in August 2026.

Suits

Buyers who want to live centrally without a car, or want a long-term asset in the most established location.

Watch for

Older buildings need a structural and services check; new builds are rare and expensive; summer crowds.

Gonio–Kvariati

$2,657/m² average, turnkey primary market · 196 sales, Jan–Aug 2026

A resort suburb about 15 km south of the centre, by the Turkish border. Premium complexes have pushed the average above most city districts. A large Eagle Hills master-planned project was announced for Gonio in 2025.

Suits

Buyers looking for a resort-style second home who will not commute daily.

Watch for

Only 196 sales in eight months, so resale can take longer; new large-scale supply could shift prices.

Alley of Heroes

$2,255/m² average, turnkey primary market · 2,485 sales, Jan–Aug 2026

A district of high-rise, business and premium residential complexes. Second only to New Boulevard by sales (2,485 in January–August 2026), at about $450 more per m².

Suits

Buyers who want a newer, higher-spec building closer to the centre than New Boulevard.

Watch for

Prices vary widely between projects; compare within the district rather than with the average.

Makhinjauri

$1,998/m² average, turnkey primary market · 779 sales, Jan–Aug 2026

A former settlement north of the centre that is now part of the city, with a quieter, suburban feel. Both residential complexes and aparthotels are being built. It grew fastest of Batumi's submarkets in 2026.

Suits

People who want to live by the sea away from the busy centre, including families.

Watch for

Commute time to the centre; rental demand is more seasonal than downtown.

New Boulevard

$1,798/m² average, turnkey primary market · 4,417 sales, Jan–Aug 2026

Part of "New Batumi", the southern extension of the seafront where most high-rise towers and aparthotels have gone up. About 3 km from the airport. It had 4,417 sales in the first eight months of 2026, roughly 40% of all registered sales in Batumi.

Suits

Investors who want a furnished unit to let, and buyers who want a sea-view new build below Old Batumi prices.

Watch for

Hundreds of near-identical studios compete for tenants and resale buyers. Check whether the unit is registered as residential or as a hotel room.

Kobuleti

$1,734/m² average, turnkey primary market · 401 sales, Jan–Aug 2026

A separate resort town north of Batumi, in its own municipality, which Galt & Taggart includes in its Batumi review. High-rise projects are going up along the main avenue near the sea, and buses run frequently to Batumi.

Suits

Buyers with a smaller budget who want a seaside apartment for summer use or seasonal letting.

Watch for

Stronger seasonality than Batumi; many projects still under construction; fewer services in winter.
