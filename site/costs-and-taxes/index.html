Costs and taxes

Buying costs in Georgia are low: there is no transfer tax and total buyer costs are usually 0.6–1.1% of the price. Owners pay 5% on residential rent, and property tax only above a family income of GEL 100,000.

Owner's taxes in 2026

TaxRateWhen it applies
Property tax0% or 0.8–1%From 1 October 2026, none if your family's income last year was GEL 100,000 or less. Above that, 0.8–1% of market value. The old GEL 40,000 threshold and the 0.05–0.2% rate were abolished.
Residential rent5%On gross rent, with no deductions, when a home is let for living in (Tax Code art. 81). The general income tax rate is 20%.
Sale after 2+ years0%Gains on a home owned for more than two years are exempt.
Sale within 2 years5%On the gain, for apartments.
Short-term letting as a sole trader1% of turnoverWith small business status and turnover up to GEL 500,000 a year. Ask the Revenue Service whether it fits your letting.
Many sites still quote the old GEL 40,000 property-tax threshold. Check current rules with the Revenue Service (rs.ge) or a tax adviser, and check how your home country taxes income from Georgia.
