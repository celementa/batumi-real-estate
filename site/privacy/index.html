Privacy notice

How we handle the data you send through the request form.

Last updated: November 2026

Draft for legal review: the legal entity, address and contact for data requests will be added before launch.

What we collect

What you enter in the request form: name, contact details, budget, area, goal and your message.

Why

So a Brik agent can contact you and send apartments that match. We do not use it for anything else without your consent.

Who sees it

The Brik team. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to answer and support your purchase, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. The main Brik site has its own privacy notice.

Analytics

We may use web analytics. Links to Brik carry tags that show the visit came from this site.
