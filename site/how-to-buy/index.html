How to buy property in Batumi

A foreigner needs only a passport to buy an apartment in Batumi. The sale is registered with the Public Registry, usually within one to several working days, and ownership passes at that moment.

Last updated: November 2026

Who can buy

Foreign individuals and companies can own apartments, houses and commercial property in Georgia on the same terms as citizens. The exception is agricultural land, which the constitution reserves for the state, municipalities and Georgian citizens. You do not need a visa, residence permit or local partner to buy.

What you need

  • Your passport.
  • A sale contract, in Georgian and usually bilingual.
  • Proof of paying the registration fee (tariff on napr.gov.ge).
  • A power of attorney with a notarised Georgian translation if someone signs for you.

Resale or off-plan?

Turnkey primary-market apartments averaged $1,865 per m² in December 2025; resale was about $1,450. New builds are often sold with developer instalments, but you take on the risk of delays.

Off-plan units are sold in stages of finish: black frame (walls, windows, entrance door only), white frame (services installed, walls plastered, rough screed) and green frame (ready for decorative finishing). What each includes varies by developer, so check the contract.

The process

  1. Check the registry

    Get an extract from the National Agency of Public Registry: owner, area, mortgage, arrest or other encumbrances.

  2. Check the developer

    For off-plan: the developer owns the land, the land is not pledged, the building permit matches the project, and earlier projects were delivered.

  3. Preliminary agreement

    Price, deposit, deadlines, penalties for delay and how the deposit is refunded. Have a lawyer read it.

  4. Bank and payment

    Pay by bank transfer to the seller's account, never in cash through an intermediary. Link off-plan payments to construction stages.

  5. Sale contract

    Signed at a notary or a Public Service Hall in front of a registrar.

  6. Registration

    Ownership passes only when the registry records it, usually within one to several working days.

There is no state scheme that protects off-plan buyers' money in Georgia. The contract, the payment schedule and the developer's track record are your protection.
