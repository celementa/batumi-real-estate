Frequently asked questions

Short answers with links to the full pages. Figures as of November 2026.

Last updated: November 2026

Can foreigners buy property in Batumi?

Yes, on the same terms as Georgian citizens. Only agricultural land is restricted. Read more

Do I need a visa or residence to buy?

No. A passport is enough. Read more

How much does an apartment cost?

New developments averaged $1,408 per m² in September 2026 (Colliers). Turnkey new builds range from $1,734 to $3,224 per m² by area. Read more

Which area sells the most?

New Boulevard: 4,417 sales in January–August 2026, about 40% of the city total. Read more

What rental yield can I expect?

About 7.1% gross (June 2026), before costs and tax. Read more

What taxes will I pay?

No transfer tax. 5% on residential rent; no tax on a sale after two years of ownership; property tax only above a family income of GEL 100,000. Read more

Does buying give me residence?

Property worth at least $150,000 lets you apply for a one-year renewable residence permit. It is not automatic. Read more

Can I buy without travelling?

Yes, through a representative with a notarised power of attorney translated into Georgian. Read more

Is off-plan safe?

There is no state guarantee for off-plan buyers. Check land ownership, the building permit and the developer's delivered projects, and tie payments to construction stages. Read more
