Buying in Batumi without travelling

You do not need to be in Georgia to buy. A representative can sign for you under a notarised power of attorney, translated into Georgian; the sale is registered in the same way.

Last updated: November 2026

How it works

You can buy through a representative holding your power of attorney. You sign it before a notary in your country; in Georgia it is translated into Georgian and the translation is notarised.

Documents from most countries need an apostille. Russian powers of attorney do not, because Russia and Georgia are both parties to the Minsk Convention.

Scope the power carefully

  • Name the specific property, or limit the price and area.
  • Say whether the representative may sign the preliminary agreement, the sale contract, or both.
  • Do not grant the right to receive your money unless you mean to.

Paying safely

  • Pay by bank transfer directly to the seller's account.
  • Ask for the registry extract before any deposit and again just before signing.
  • Get the signed contract and the new registry extract once ownership is registered.
  • Use a representative who is independent of the seller or developer.
Some buyers watch a video viewing before signing. A video call shows the flat, but not the building's services or the land title; the registry check still matters.
