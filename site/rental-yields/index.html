Rental yields in Batumi

Batumi apartments earned a gross yield of about 7.1% a year in June 2026 (Galt & Taggart). Individuals pay 5% on residential rent; nightly letting and hotel-room units follow different rules.

A worked example

A 40 m² apartment at average rents brings in about $424–492 a month, or $5,100–5,900 a year before costs. After utilities, repairs, management, vacancy and 5% tax, the net yield is lower than the 7.1% gross figure.

Gross yields have fallen from 8.8% in 2024. In large complexes with hundreds of similar studios, competition for guests and resale buyers is strong.

Aparthotels and management contracts

Many complexes sell units with a management agreement: the operator lets the unit and splits income with the owner. Before buying, ask for the operator's share, who pays for repairs and furniture, the contract term and how to exit early. A developer's projected yield is a forecast, not a guarantee.

Check in the registry extract whether the unit is registered as a residence or as a non-residential hotel room. It changes how rental income is taxed.
